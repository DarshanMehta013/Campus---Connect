The 4-Step Operational Resolution Portal

How an issue moves seamlessly from student submission to verified campus clearance.

1

Student Report & Hazard Scan

Student uploads video/photo evidence. Automated engine scans keywords for electrical/physical risks and auto-escalates priority.

2

Admin Audit & Verification

Principal verifier reviews report validity, filters potential spam, and routes ticket to the appropriate faculty office.

3

Faculty Work Order Dispatch

Department Faculty selects an active technician, assigns resolution deadline, and tracks repair execution.

4

Technician Fix & QA Inspection

Technician completes task and uploads photograph proof. Faculty inspects and marks ticket as perfectly completed.
